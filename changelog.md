# v1.0.0

First release.

- Startpos switcher: rebindable keys, one restart per burst of presses, and a saved state per startpos.
- The `< 4/21 >` switcher display, with clickable arrows.
- Works with startposes you place while playing, using GD 2.209's own tool.
- Go to: press G and type a startpos number.
- Fast SP: prepare every startpos before the level starts.
- Smart startpos: speed, gamemode, size, gravity, dual and mirror follow the level's portals. Gravity after orbs and pads is learned from your runs.
- Linked levels: link a Practice copy to its Target level and swap between them. Seamless swap and Keep Practice loaded are optional.
- Settings: default startpos for all levels or per level. Every setting applies without reopening the level.
